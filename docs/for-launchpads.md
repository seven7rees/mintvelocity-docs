# For launchpads and partners

Mintvelocity gives external projects and branded launch environments (such as based.bid's projects and Boards) a Solana launch venue where hooks are first-class.

## What you get

- **Programmable fee mechanics wired into the token and the pool**, not a menu of preset fee splits. A hook can cut, burn, reward and lock based on the trade itself.
- **A growing menu of hooks written by outside developers.** New mechanics show up without your team building them.
- **Builders with money on the line.** Hook builders earn a share when launches use their hook, so they promote the launches that use it. Every launch gets extra push beyond its own community.
- **Creators on your side earn too.** The creator fee is set at launch and routed by rules they pick.

## Integration surface

The TypeScript SDK builds every instruction of the token program, the DEX, the bridge and the launchpad, decodes every account and event, and resolves a hook's accounts.

Mintvelocity is pre-launch (see the roadmap). Partnership mechanics are open for discussion now.
