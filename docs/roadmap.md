# Status and roadmap

## Live and proven (on the original platform's deployment of this stack)

- The full stack ran on Solana mainnet from October 7, 2026, open source under Apache-2.0
- The FUEL hook earned builder fees on its first launch (see the case study)
- 31 SOL paid to 8 hook builders over 9 days across 30 coins (platform-reported, October 17, 2026)

## Built, deploying next (Mintvelocity)

- Full site, backend and indexer, building clean
- Public brand: mintvelocity.com and @mintvelocity
- Next step: devnet deploy and test, then mainnet under Mintvelocity's own program keys

## Coming after launch

- Native SOL launches, removing the bridge step: rebuilt behind an off switch, devnet-tested, with an outside Rust review before mainnet
- Fee-partner features

## Not final until launch

- The protocol fee schedule and submit costs (current numbers are the stack's shipped defaults)
- Mintvelocity's program addresses, generated at deploy
